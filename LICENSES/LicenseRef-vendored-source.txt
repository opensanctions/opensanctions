Vendored source material — no license granted by OpenSanctions

Files marked with the SPDX identifier `LicenseRef-vendored-source` are unmodified reproductions
of material published by a third party. They are committed to this repository for provenance and
so that changes at the source can be reviewed as a diff. They fall into two groups:

  * artifacts downloaded and committed as retrieved — source PDFs, saved HTML pages, and
    extracted-text dumps of source PDFs; and
  * snapshots that a crawler writes back verbatim on every run, so that a maintainer can see what
    changed upstream.

files, and grants no license to them. Whatever rights exist remain with the original publisher.
Anyone wishing to reuse one of these files must assess it against that publisher's own terms.

of a government body and may be in the public domain, or subject to a public sector information
or open data regime, in their jurisdiction — but this varies by publisher and by country, and it
is the reuser's responsibility to establish.
